Support

Parlay Tracker is a personal notebook for bets you place elsewhere: live win probability, automatic grading and an honest look at your results. It doesn't accept wagers or connect to any sportsbook.

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea?

Email and you'll get a reply. If a slip didn't scan right, attach the photo or screenshot; it's the fastest way to get it fixed.

Common questions

A slip scanned with a wrong leg or price.
Fix it on the review screen before you add it, or tap the leg on the ticket later and choose Edit. The review screen warns you when the legs don't multiply out to the payout printed on the slip.
A leg isn't grading on its own.
Legs grade from ESPN's live scores once their game is matched. A leg the app can't match or can't score, such as an unusual prop, shows Hit and Miss buttons so you can settle it yourself.
My tickets aren't showing up on my other devices.
Make sure each device is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for Parlay Tracker (Settings, your name, iCloud). Sync can take a minute or two.
The Lock Screen or Watch hasn't updated.
Live Activities and the Watch update when the app refreshes. Opening Parlay Tracker brings them up to date.
How do I back up or move my tickets?
Use the export in the app to save a backup file, then import it on another device. iCloud sync also keeps a copy in your own iCloud.

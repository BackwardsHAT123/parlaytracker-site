Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Parlay Tracker is a personal notebook for tracking bets you place somewhere else. It does not accept wagers, process bets, or connect to any sportsbook account. Because it does so little with your data, this policy is short.

What stays on your device (and your own iCloud)

Everything you enter into Parlay Tracker (tickets, legs, stakes, notes, tags) is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for the app, that data syncs through your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit) to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. We never see this data, have no server of our own that stores it, and cannot access it.

Game Boards (squares) are stored on the device where you made them and are not synced. Widgets, the Apple Watch app and Live Activities get their information from the app on your own devices.

What we don't collect

  • No user accounts, sign-ups or logins of our own
  • No analytics or usage-tracking code
  • No advertising networks or advertising identifiers
  • No data sold, rented or shared with third parties
  • No server operated by us that stores your bet history

Scanning a slip

When you scan a slip, whether a photo you take, a picture you choose, or a screenshot you share to the app, the text is read on your device with Apple's Vision framework to fill in a ticket. The image and its text are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Scanning a squares board works the same way.

Live scores, odds and weather

To show scores, schedules, odds, rosters, team logos, win probability and game-day weather, the app requests publicly available sports data from ESPN. These requests ask only for public game information. They don't include your bets or anything that identifies you, and they aren't tied to an account.

If you choose to enter your own key for The Odds API (used by Check Market and the preferred-sportsbook option), the app sends that key and the sport you're looking at to The Odds API to fetch prices. Your key is stored in your device's keychain. Nothing about your bets is sent.

Sharing a ticket

When you share a ticket as a picture or animated GIF, whether in Messages or through the share sheet, the image is made on your device and goes only where you send it.

Siri and Shortcuts

The Siri and Shortcuts actions, such as Check My Tickets and Scan a Bet Slip, run on your device and read the same on-device data as the app.

Tip jar

Tips are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Your payment information is handled by Apple and never passes through or is stored by Parlay Tracker.

This website

parlaytracker.app uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other companies' servers. The web host and its network provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Children

Parlay Tracker is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under the legal age for sports betting where they live.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
